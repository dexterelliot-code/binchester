2. What the app does

Binchester helps you review the photos and videos on your phone and delete the ones you don't want. You swipe left to put an item in the app's bin and right to keep it. Nothing is deleted until you review the bin and confirm in your phone's own delete dialog.

  • Binchester works entirely on your phone. It never uploads your photos or videos. If you use iCloud Photos, that is Apple syncing your library, not Binchester. We don't collect any data about you. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Suggestions are only suggestions. The app may group similar photos, suggest a "best shot" or show photos that look blurry. It never puts anything in the bin for you, and it never suggests your favourites for the bin.

3. Deleting is your decision

You choose what to delete, and you confirm every deletion in your phone's system dialog. Please check the bin before you confirm.

  • On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days before the phone removes them. If you use iCloud Photos, they are removed from all your devices.
  • On Android, deleting is permanent. Deleted items don't go to a bin you can restore them from.

We can't recover anything you have deleted. On iPhone you can get items back yourself from Recently Deleted in the Photos app within 30 days. Keep a backup of anything you care about.
