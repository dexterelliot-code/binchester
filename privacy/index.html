Binchester Privacy Policy

App: Binchester (iPhone and Android)
Publisher: CouplaQuid
Effective date: 9 November 2026
Contact: couplaquid@gmail.com

The short version

Binchester does not collect any data about you. Your photos stay on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

What the app does with your photos

Binchester needs access to your photo library to show you your photos and videos, so you can decide what to keep and what to delete.

  • Everything happens on your phone. Photos and videos are read from your phone's photo library and are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared with anyone, including us.
  • To find similar and blurry photos, the app makes small thumbnails and calculates simple measurements (such as how sharp a photo is) on your phone. These stay on your phone.
  • The app only deletes photos and videos you have chosen and then confirmed in your phone's own delete dialog.
  • If you give the app access to only some of your photos, it only sees those.

On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. On Android, deleting is permanent.

What is stored on your phone

To remember your progress, the app stores a small amount of information in its own storage on your phone:

  • which photos you have kept or put in the bin, identified by the phone's internal photo ids
  • what is waiting in the bin
  • your deletion counts and space-freed totals
  • whether you have bought the unlock for unlimited deletions
  • scan results for similar and blurry photos

This information is not sent to us. It stays on your phone, and in your phone's own backup (such as iCloud Backup, Google's device backup or a backup on your computer) if you use one. You can clear your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress. Deleting the app removes it from your phone.

Purchases

If you buy the unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To check whether you own the unlock, the app asks the App Store or Google Play on your phone for your purchase record. That record stays on your phone. The app uses an open-source in-app purchase library (expo-iap, from the OpenIAP project) that talks only to Apple's and Google's purchase services. We do not use any purchase-verification server.

  • Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution tools.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell data. There is no data to sell.

If you email us

The app itself sends us nothing. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and nothing else.

  • We use them only to reply to you and to deal with your request. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • We don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share your email with anyone.
  • Your emails are stored with our email provider. We delete a conversation 12 months after the last message, unless we need to keep it longer for a legal reason.

For these emails, CouplaQuid is the controller under UK data protection law.

This website

The pages that hold these documents set no cookies and use no analytics. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log your IP address to keep its service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Children

Binchester is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The app doesn't send us any personal data, so there is nothing about your use of the app for us to access, correct or delete. All of the app's data is on your phone and under your control.

If you have emailed us, you can ask us for a copy of those emails, or ask us to delete them, by writing to the address below. If you're unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it at the same address and change the effective date above. If a change affects what the app does with data, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
