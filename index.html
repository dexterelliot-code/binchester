Binchester

Binchester is a photo clean-up app for iPhone, published by CouplaQuid. Swipe through your camera roll, group similar shots and clear the blurry ones. It works entirely on your phone and never uploads your photos.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email couplaquid@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Purchases and refunds

To get the unlock back on a new phone, sign in with the same Apple Account or Google account and tap "Restore purchases" in the app. Refunds are handled by the App Store (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.
